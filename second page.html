Frequently Asked Questions

How does "Food as Medicine" work in daily life? ▼
Whole foods contain synergistic combinations of vitamins, minerals, dietary fibers, and phytochemicals (such as flavonoids, carotenoids, and glucosinolates) that support cellular repair, modulate inflammatory pathways, and nourish gut microbiome health.
Why does the sleep calculator use 90-minute cycles? ▼
Human sleep naturally progresses through ultradian cycles consisting of NREM (stages 1, 2, and slow-wave deep sleep) and REM sleep. Completing full 90-minute cycles minimizes "sleep inertia"—the groggy feeling caused by waking up mid-deep sleep.
Is BMI alone enough to judge health? ▼
BMI is a helpful screening metric for population weight ranges, but it does not measure muscle mass, bone density, or visceral fat distribution. Combining BMI with waist circumference and clinical biomarker blood tests provides a complete picture.
How frequently is the research database updated? ▼
Our research directory is updated continuously as major clinical guidelines and peer-reviewed journals publish updated dietary recommendations and nutritional biochemistry papers.
